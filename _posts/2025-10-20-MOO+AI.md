# MOO+AI

The rapid advancement of technology, exemplified by developments such as Multi-Object Optimization, AI-assisted drawing, and parametric integration with Building Information Modeling (BIM), foreshadows an impending revolution within the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) industry. As projects grow in complexity, clients' demands for expeditious completion exert significant pressure on professionals. Moreover, the dynamic nature of the market compels smaller firms to strategically distance themselves from large-scale project competition, due to the associated risks and budgetary considerations.

The following illustration serves as an exemplar of a workflow that amalgamates these technological methodologies into the daily operations of architects. This approach seeks to rectify the prevailing challenges, wherein architects aim to maintain competitiveness with major industry players while minimizing the reliance on manual labor.

As opposed to metaheuristic algorithms requiring an explicit objective function (such as Galapagos), Interactive Evolutionary Algorithms (IEAs) allow designers to engage with the process of evolutionary development itself. This gives rise to an involved experience, helping to explore the wide combinatorial space of parametric models without always knowing where you are headed!

---

The final phase involves parametric Building Information Modeling (BIM) modeling. In many instances, especially during the later stages of the design process, such as project development or the creation of construction documents, implementing design changes can be a labor-intensive endeavor. This is further exacerbated when coordinating changes across various professional disciplines. However, the integration of Parametric Tools such as Rhino.Inside.Revit or Dynamo can significantly alleviate this challenge. When the modeling logic is predefined, it affords the flexibility to modify the design when certain requirements are not met.

Furthermore, proficiency in scripting and programming can enhance cost calculations and building process simulations. This capability empowers users to continually enrich the model with additional value, thus optimizing the overall design and construction process.

---

Leveraging Biomorpher, which is seamlessly integrated with Multi-Object Optimization, design choices remain confined primarily to the select elite configurations within the Pareto front. In this specific scenario, the evaluation encompasses factors such as incident solar radiation and the potential maximization of the building's volume on the site. Through the utilization of the Grasshopper plugin Ladybug, users can readily facilitate an intuitive comparison among the candidate designs.

Conversely, the adoption of AI-assisted drawing techniques, which have gained significant popularity in recent years, can facilitate consensus between designers and clients regarding the design direction. It also furnishes compelling architectural visualizations, even with rudimentary volumetric models or simple sketches. This transformative capability has the potential to fundamentally alter the acquisition process during the early design stages.
